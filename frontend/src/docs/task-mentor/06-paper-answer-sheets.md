---
title: Use paper answer sheets for a quiz
summary: Print an answer sheet for each student, photograph the completed sheets and save the results to the quiz.
---

Paper answer sheets let students answer a quiz on paper. They fill in bubbles, you photograph the sheets, and Task Mentor reads the answers and scores them.

## Before you start

- The quiz must already have its questions. If you change the questions after printing, you must print new sheets.
- Not every question fits on a bubble sheet. The page tells you which questions are marked by hand; students answer those on their own answer paper.
- One sheet holds up to 75 questions.

## Print the sheets

1. Open the quiz and select **Paper answer sheets**.
2. Stay on the **1. Print** tab.
3. Tick the students who need a sheet. **Select all** and **Clear all** change the whole list.
4. Select the **Print** button. It shows the number of sheets.
5. In the print window, print at 100% (no "fit to page"), one sided.

Each sheet carries the student's name and a QR code, so the sheets can be handed in in any order.

> **Important:** Ask students to fill one bubble per question completely with a dark pen or pencil, and not to fold the sheet or write near the corners. They fill several bubbles only where a question says "choose all".

## Scan and save

1. Open the **2. Scan and save** tab.
2. Select **Add photos or scans** and choose the pictures. On a phone, this opens the camera.
3. Photograph each sheet flat, from above, with all four corner squares in the picture.
4. Check the answers Task Mentor read. Answers it is unsure about are highlighted with a label: **Blank**, **Two marked** or **Faint mark**.
5. Select a bubble to correct an answer.
6. Select the **Save** button. It shows the number of results.

After saving, each student is listed with a score and percentage.

> **Tip:** If a photo is poor, take it again. A new scan of the same student replaces the earlier one.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Couldn't find the four corner squares" | Photograph the whole sheet, flat and well lit |
| "Couldn't read the QR code" | Keep the top-right corner of the sheet sharp and uncovered |
| "This sheet belongs to another quiz" | Open the correct quiz, then scan the sheet there |
| "Printed before the quiz's questions changed: print new sheets" | Print new sheets from the **1. Print** tab |
