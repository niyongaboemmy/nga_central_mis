---
title: Getting around Task Mentor
summary: Learn what Task Mentor is for, how to open it, and where to find your subjects, work to grade and reports.
---

Task Mentor is the Amashuri app for setting work and recording results: assignments, quizzes, grading, marks and report cards.

As a teacher you set work for your subjects, grade it, record marks from paper tests, prepare report cards and follow each student's progress.

## Open Task Mentor

Open Task Mentor from your list of Amashuri apps. You sign in with your Amashuri account. The app opens on your **Dashboard**.

## The menu

The **Menus** panel on the left lists your pages. On a phone, tap the menu button in the top bar. What you see depends on your role.

| Menu item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Your day at a glance: work to grade, averages and deadlines |
| **Courses** | The subjects you teach, each with its own tabs |
| **Assignments** | Create and manage assignments |
| **Quizzes** | Create and manage quizzes |
| **To grade** | Everything waiting for a grade |
| **Students** | Students in your subjects |
| **Question Bank** | Reusable quiz questions per subject |
| **Grades** | Recorded marks and report card mapping |
| **Report cards** | The report card workflow for each class |

## Choose the academic year and term

Task Mentor shows one period (an academic year and a term) at a time.

1. In the top bar, select the period button (it shows the current year and term).
2. Under **Academic year & term**, pick a year, then a term.

> **Note:** On a phone, the period button is at the bottom of the menu.

## The dashboard

The dashboard shows tiles such as **To grade**, **Class average**, **Pass rate** and **Due in 7 days**, then your **Grading queue**, **Upcoming deadlines** and **Subject scorecards**. Select a subject to focus the dashboard on it.

## Open a subject

1. Select **Courses**.
2. Search by name or code, then select the subject.
3. Use the tabs: **Overview**, **Assignments**, **Quizzes**, **Recorded Assessments**, **Students**, **Ranking**, **Question Bank** and **Report Cards**. Tabs that do not fit are under **More**.

## The top bar

- The grid button (**View Applications**) opens your other Amashuri apps.
- The bell shows your **Notifications**.
- Your account menu has **Profile** and **Sign Out**.

## If something goes wrong

- A page looks empty: check the period in the top bar. Work from another term is hidden.
- You see "You have no assigned subjects this term": ask your school administrator to assign your subjects in Amashuri.
