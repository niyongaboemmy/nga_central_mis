title: Follow your students' progress
summary: Find a student, read their profile and see how a class ranks and who needs support.
---

Task Mentor brings each student's assignments, quizzes and recorded marks together, so you can spot who needs help early.

## Before you start

Check the year and term in the top bar. Averages, rankings and profiles all follow the selected period.

## Find a student

1. Select **Students**. The **My Students** page lists everyone enrolled in the subjects you teach this term.
2. Group the list **By subject** or **By class group**, or search by name or email.
3. Select a student to open their profile.

## Read a student's profile

The top of the profile shows the student's **Average**, **Class rank** and how many items need **Attention**.

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Overall average, work marked, quiz pass rate and performance per subject |
| **Assignments** | Each assignment, with filters such as **Needs attention** and **Awaiting marking** |
| **Recorded Assessments** | Marks you recorded by hand, such as class work and midterms |
| **Report Cards** | The student's report cards |

## See the class ranking

1. Select **Overall Ranking**.
2. Choose a **Subject**, or keep **All subjects**.
3. Under **Kind of work**, choose **All work**, **Assignments**, **Quizzes** or **Recorded**.
4. Group the leaderboard as **One list**, **By class** or **By grade**.

How the ranking works:

- Only marked work counts: graded assignments, completed quizzes and marks recorded in class.
- Work that is not handed in or not marked yet never counts as a zero.
- A quiz counts the best completed attempt.

## Check one subject

1. Select **Courses** and open the subject.
2. On the **Overview** tab, read **At a glance**: class average, pass rate, students at risk and how much marking is done.
3. Look at **Students to support** and **Top performers**.
4. Select **Report** for the full subject report.

## School-wide reports

If your role allows it, **School reports** in the menu shows the **Broadsheet**, **Performance**, **Gender**, **Trends** and **At risk** sections for a term. Filter by programme or class.

## If something goes wrong

- The leaderboard says "No marked work yet": students appear once an assignment, quiz or recorded assessment is marked for the selected period.
- A student seems to be missing: check the period in the top bar.
