---
title: Draft a grade with AI
summary: Let an AI propose scores and feedback from your criteria, then review them and save the grade yourself.
---

Use this guide to speed up grading. An AI reads the student's work and your criteria, and fills in the grade form. Nothing is saved until you save it.

## Before you start

- TMCode AI must be available to you. Your school decides this.
- Open a submission from a student who has handed in. See the guide on grading a student's code.

## Draft with TMCode AI

1. In the grade panel, choose **Draft with AI…**.
2. If you want, tell the AI what to focus on, for example "check input validation".
3. Wait for the draft. The scores, a note for each criterion and the feedback appear in the form.
4. Read **Notes for you**. This is where the AI explains its draft.
5. Check every score and change what you disagree with. Each score and note carries an AI mark until you change it.
6. Save with **Save & Next**, **Save Draft** or **Save & Release**, as you do for any grade.

> **Important:** The AI only proposes. You are the grader. Read the code yourself before you save.

## Throw a draft away

Choose **Discard AI Draft**. The form goes back to how it was before the draft.

## Use another AI tool

Where your school allows other AI tools for staff, **More Actions** offers more choices:

- **Grade with Claude Code**, and the same for a few other AI tools. A terminal opens and the tool works on the grading task. If the tool is not installed, TMCode says how to install it.
- **Copy Grading Prompt** copies the grading task, so you can paste it into an AI extension's chat panel.

The draft lands in the same form.

## Check that the student understands their code

1. Open the student's submission.
2. Open the command palette and run **Explain-Your-Code Questions (Grading)**.
3. TMCode AI writes a few questions about the student's own code, with what a good answer would mention.
4. Ask the student the questions yourself and judge the answers.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Pick a student who handed in, then Draft with AI."** No submission is open. Pick a student whose work is waiting to be graded.
- **The draft is refused.** TMCode checks every draft. If it is broken, TMCode says what was wrong and your form does not change. Try again.
- **Draft with AI asks you to update Task Mentor.** Your school's Task Mentor is too old for this feature. Ask your school administrator.
