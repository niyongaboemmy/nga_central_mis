---
title: Grade a student's code
summary: Open submissions, score them against your criteria, comment on lines, and release grades or return work.
---

Use this guide to grade code assignments and coding questions in TMCode, with the student's code next to the grade form.

## Before you start

- Sign in with your school account. You see the work of your own subjects.

## Open the work

1. On **Home**, choose **Start Grading**. TMCode opens the oldest waiting submission.
2. To grade one assignment only, open **Assignments** and choose **Grade** on its row.

The student's code opens with the grade panel beside it. The switcher at the top moves between students.

## Grade one student

1. Read the code. To see what the student changed, look under **Files and changes**.
2. Under **Criteria**, give a score for each criterion. If the work has no criteria, you see one **Final grade** box.
3. Under **Feedback**, write what went well and what to improve.
4. To comment on a line, point at the line in the code and choose **+**. Your comments are listed under **Line comments**.
5. Choose **Save & Next**. TMCode saves and opens the next waiting student.

## Choose when students see the grade

The arrow beside the save button offers two more ways to save:

- **Save Draft** keeps the grade to yourself. The student does not see it yet.
- **Save & Release** saves and shows the grade to the student.

When you have drafts, a **Release Drafts** button at the top, with the number of drafts, shows them all to their students at once.

> **Note:** For a quiz, students see the grades when the quiz results are released.

## Return work for changes

1. Open **More Actions** and choose **Return for Changes…**.
2. Write what to change before submitting again.
3. Choose **Return to Student**.

Work that is already graded can be reopened with **Allow Resubmission**. Quiz practicals cannot be returned.

## More tools

**More Actions** also has:

- **Run the Student's Unit Tests**, to run the tests in the handed-in version;
- **Preview the Website**, for web work;
- **Compare with Version…**, to compare with an earlier version the student saved.

When you finish, choose **Close Review** to go back to the folder you had open.

## If something goes wrong

- **A student's row is marked "Not saved".** The save failed. Your scores are kept on this computer. Choose **Retry**.
- **Another teacher graded the same student while you worked.** TMCode shows their grade and asks you to choose **Use Theirs** or **Keep Mine and Save**.
