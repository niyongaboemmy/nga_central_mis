title: Move between the apps
summary: Use the app tabs, the search box and the title bar buttons to get around quickly.
---

This guide shows how to move between Amashuri, Task Mentor, Tendo and Tupo inside Amashuri Desktop.

## Open an app

The title bar (the bar at the top of the window) has one tab per app. A number on a tab shows unread items in that app.

1. Click a tab to open that app.
2. Or press Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+4 on Windows, or Cmd+1 to Cmd+4 on a Mac.
3. Press Ctrl+Tab to go to the next app.

Right-click a tab for more choices: **Open**, **Reload**, **Go to Start Page**, **Open in Browser** and **Mute Notifications**.

## Jump to a page with search

1. Click **Search or jump to…** in the title bar, or press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac).
2. Type a few letters, for example "quizzes", "attendance" or "calculator".
3. Choose a result. It can be an app, a page inside an app, a tool or an action.

## The title bar buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Back** and **Forward** | Move through the pages you visited in the current app |
| **Reload** | Loads the current app's page again |
| **Tools** | Opens the tools launcher |
| **Notifications** | Opens the list of notifications from every app |
| **Appearance** | Lets you choose a light or dark look |
| **Keyboard shortcuts** | Lists every shortcut |
| **More** | Print, open in browser, downloads and focus mode |
| **Settings** | Opens the app's settings |

Point at any button to see its name and shortcut.

## Useful extras

- **Focus mode** hides the title bar so one app fills the window. Press Ctrl+Shift+F (Cmd+Shift+F on a Mac) to enter, and click **Exit focus** to leave.
- **Zoom.** Press Ctrl and = to zoom in, Ctrl and - to zoom out, and Ctrl+0 for the actual size. On a Mac, use Cmd.
- **One theme everywhere.** Under **Settings**, **Appearance**, choose **Light**, **Dark** or **This computer**. The choice also applies to the four apps.
- **Start app.** Under **Settings**, **General**, use **Start with** to pick the app that opens first, or **The app I used last**.
- **Language.** Under **Settings**, **General**, use **Language** to choose English, French or Kinyarwanda.

> **Tip:** Closing the window does not stop the app. It keeps running so notifications still arrive. To stop it fully, use its icon in the tray (Windows) or the menu bar (Mac).

## If something goes wrong

- **A page looks old or stuck.** Click **Reload**.
- **An app shows "You're offline".** Check the Wi-Fi or network cable, then click **Try again**.
