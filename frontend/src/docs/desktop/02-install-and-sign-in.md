title: Install the app and sign in
summary: Download Amashuri Desktop, sign in once, switch school and sign out safely.
---

This guide gets Amashuri Desktop onto your computer and signs you in to all the school apps at once.

## Before you start

- You need a Windows or Mac computer and an internet connection.
- You need your usual Amashuri account.

## Install the app

1. In your browser, open the Apps page at `https://mis.amashuri.com/apps`. The page detects whether you use Windows or Mac.
2. Download Amashuri Desktop and open the downloaded file.
3. Follow the installer, then start the app.

## Sign in

1. Open any app tab. If nobody is signed in, the tab shows a sign-in button.
2. Click it and sign in with your Amashuri account.
3. Wait a moment. Task Mentor, Tendo and Tupo sign in by themselves in the background.

If you sign in with Google, the app shows **Continue in your browser** and opens your browser. Choose your Google account there, then return to the app. If the browser tab did not appear, click **Open the browser again**.

On first start, a strip asks about notifications. Click **Turn on** to get banners for messages, reminders and deadlines.

## Switch school

If you belong to two or more schools, the title bar shows a chip with the current school's name and logo.

1. Click the school chip. Its tooltip reads **Switch school**.
2. Choose the other school in Amashuri.

You stay signed in. The other apps sign in again into the new school, and the notification list shows that school's notices.

## Sign out

1. Click the gear button to open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Account** and click **Sign out of this computer**.
3. Click **Yes, sign out everywhere**.

This ends your session in every school app and removes your data from the computer.

> **Important:** Always sign out on a shared computer. To also delete your notes, flashcards, AI chats and saved class lists, turn on **Remove my tool data when I sign out** under **Account** first.

## If something goes wrong

- **A Mac refuses to open the app.** Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway.
- **The Google sign-in never finishes.** Click **Cancel**, then start the sign-in again.
- **An app tab asks you to sign in again.** Sign in to Amashuri first. The other apps follow.
