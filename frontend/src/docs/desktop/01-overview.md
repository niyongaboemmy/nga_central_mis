---
title: What Amashuri Desktop is
summary: Learn what Amashuri Desktop does and when to use it instead of your browser.
---

Amashuri Desktop is one program for Windows and Mac that opens all your school apps in a single window, so you do not need a browser tab for each one.

## What you get

- **One window, four apps.** Amashuri, Task Mentor, Tendo and Tupo each have a tab in the bar at the top of the window (the title bar).
- **One sign-in.** You sign in once, in Amashuri. The other apps then open already signed in.
- **Notifications from every app.** Messages, reminders and deadlines appear as banners on your computer and are collected under the bell button, even when the window is closed.
- **Search across the apps.** One search box jumps to an app, a page, a tool or an action.
- **Tools.** A set of everyday and classroom tools such as a calculator, timers, a name picker and a whiteboard. Most of them work without internet.
- **Updates by themselves.** The app installs new versions on its own.
- **Three languages.** The app is in English, French and Kinyarwanda.

## How it differs from the browser

The apps inside Amashuri Desktop are the same live apps you use in a browser. Your data is the same in both places, and a change made to a web app reaches Amashuri Desktop as soon as you reload the page.

| In the browser | In Amashuri Desktop |
|---|---|
| One tab per app, easy to lose | One fixed tab per app |
| Each app opens on its own | You sign in once and every app follows |
| Notifications come from each app separately | Notifications from all apps in one list, kept for 7 days |
| No built-in tools | Tools for teaching and office work |

## What still works the same

- Files you download go to your Downloads folder.
- Printing works from any page.
- Camera, microphone, meetings and voice messages work inside the app.
- A link to a site outside the school apps opens in your normal browser.
