This guide explains how Amashuri Desktop updates itself and what to do when an app will not load, sign in or notify you.

## How updates work

The app looks for a new version about every hour. A new version downloads in the background and installs by itself when nobody has used the computer for 10 minutes, or when you quit the app. It never installs during a quiz, a meeting, a running timer or a tool shown full screen.

The school apps inside the window update separately. Click **Reload** to get their latest version.

## Update now

1. When a new version is ready, an **Update** button appears in the title bar. Click it.
2. Wait while the button shows the progress. The app downloads the update, installs it and restarts.

You can also update from the settings.

1. Click the gear button to open **Settings**, then go to **Updates**.
2. Click **Check for updates**.
3. If a version is ready, click **Restart & update**.

To stop automatic installs, turn off **Install updates automatically** under **Updates**.

> **Note:** If a quiz or meeting is open, the update waits. Finish it first, because updating restarts the app.

## If something goes wrong

- **An app shows "You're offline".** Check the Wi-Fi or network cable, then click **Try again**. The title bar shows **Offline** while there is no connection. The apps reconnect on their own.
- **An app is taking a while to open.** The school network or the server may be slow. Click **Try again**, or click **Open in browser** to use the app in your browser for now.
- **An app keeps showing an old page or will not sign in.** Open **Settings**, go to **Troubleshooting**, click the reset button, then click **Yes, reset**. This clears everything the app saved on the computer, so you sign in again afterwards.
- **You cannot find a downloaded file.** Open **Settings**, go to **Troubleshooting** and click **Open Downloads folder**.
- **No notification banners.** Open **Settings**, go to **Notifications** and click **Send a test**. If nothing appears, click **System settings** and allow notifications for the app.
- **The update check fails.** The message reads "Couldn't reach the update service. Try again later." Check your internet and try again later.
- **The app seems closed but notifications still arrive.** Closing the window keeps the app running. Open it again from its icon in the tray (Windows) or the menu bar (Mac).

> **Tip:** When you ask for help, open **Settings** and scroll to **About**. It shows the app version and your system, which your school administrator may ask for.
