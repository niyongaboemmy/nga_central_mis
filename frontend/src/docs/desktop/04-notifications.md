---
title: Manage notifications
summary: Read, snooze and silence notifications from all the school apps in one place.
---

This guide shows how to keep up with messages, reminders and deadlines from Amashuri, Task Mentor, Tendo and Tupo without opening each app.

## Before you start

Your computer must allow the app to show notifications. On first start, click **Turn on** in the strip at the top. You can also do this later under **Settings**, **Notifications**.

## Read your notifications

A new notification appears as a banner on your computer. All of them are also collected in one list and kept for 7 days.

1. Click the bell button in the title bar. The **Notifications** panel opens beside the app.
2. Click **All**, or an app's name, to filter the list.
3. Click a notification to open it in its app.
4. Click **Mark all read** to clear the unread count, or **Clear all** to empty the list.

## Be reminded later

1. In the **Notifications** panel, click the clock button on a notification. Its tooltip reads **Remind me later**.
2. Choose 10 min, 1 h or 3 h.

## Silence notifications for a while

1. Click the gear button to open **Settings**.
2. Under **Notifications**, next to **Do Not Disturb:**, click **1 hour** or **Until tomorrow 08:00**.
3. To end it early, click **Turn off**.

Banners also wait by themselves while you are in a Tupo meeting or a Task Mentor quiz is open. Nothing is lost. Everything still collects under the bell.

## Mute one app

1. Open **Settings** and go to **Notifications**.
2. Turn off the switch next to the app you want to silence.

You can also right-click an app tab and choose **Mute Notifications**.

## Get notifications when the window is closed

Under **Settings**, **General**, two switches control this. One starts the app quietly when the computer starts. The other keeps the app running when its window is closed. With both on, banners and timer alarms reach you without opening the app first.

> **Note:** On a shared computer, your notifications are removed when you sign out, when another person signs in and when you switch school.

## If something goes wrong

- **No banners appear.** Open **Settings**, **Notifications**, and click **Send a test**. If nothing shows, click **System settings** and allow notifications for the app on your computer.
- **The panel says the computer is blocking notifications.** Click **Open settings** and allow them.
- **Banners stopped for a while.** Check whether Do Not Disturb is on, or whether a meeting or quiz is open.
