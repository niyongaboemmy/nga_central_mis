Amashuri Desktop includes a set of tools for teaching and office work, so you do not need separate programs for a timer, a whiteboard or a PDF.

## Before you start

Sign in to Amashuri first. Some tools keep your own data, such as notes and class lists, and stay locked until the app knows who you are. The classroom tools appear for teachers and staff.

## Open a tool

1. Click the wrench button in the title bar. Its tooltip reads **Tools**. You can also press Ctrl+Shift+T (Cmd+Shift+T on a Mac).
2. Type in **Search tools…**, or pick a tool from the list.
3. Click **All tools** to go back to the list.

A tool keeps its state when you close it and open it again.

## Show a tool to the class

- Click **Open in its own window (stays on top)** to keep a tool visible while you work in an app.
- Click **Present full screen (projector)** to fill the screen. Press Esc to leave.
- Click **Add to favourites** to keep a tool under **Favourites**.

## The tools

| Group | Tools |
|---|---|
| **Everyday** | **My Day**, **Calculator**, **Timers**, **Focus timer**, **Notes**, **Unit converter**, **Date calculator**, **QR code** |
| **AI** | **Ask AI** |
| **Classroom** | **Name picker**, **Group maker**, **Noise meter**, **Work-mode signs**, **Classroom screen**, **Class game time**, **Whiteboard** |
| **Study** | **Grade calculator**, **Graphing calculator**, **Periodic table**, **Formula sheets**, **Flashcards** |
| **Games** | **Brain breaks** |
| **Office** | **PDF tools**, **Scanner** |

A few worth knowing:

- **My Day** shows today's lessons, office hours, quizzes, meetings and events, with what is now and next.
- **Name picker** and **Group maker** use your class lists from Amashuri, or lists you make yourself.
- **Timers** ring on time even when the tools are closed or the app is minimised.
- **PDF tools** and **Scanner** work on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, and files are saved to Downloads.
- **Ask AI** helps you plan a lesson, write quiz questions or draft a message. It needs internet.

> **Important:** Do not paste students' personal details into **Ask AI**. Answers can be wrong, so check important facts.

Your notes, flashcards, AI chats and saved class lists are stored encrypted on the computer, in your own space.

## If something goes wrong

- **A tool is locked.** Sign in to Amashuri, then open the tool again.
- **Ask AI says AI is off.** Your school, your role or a teacher setting has turned AI off. Ask your school administrator.
- **A tool shows "This tool stopped working."** Click **Reload**.
