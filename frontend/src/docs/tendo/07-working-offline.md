---
title: Take registers without a connection
summary: Keep taking registers when the internet drops and make sure they are sent afterwards.
---

Classroom Wi-Fi can drop. Tendo lets you keep taking registers without a connection: each register is saved on your device and sent when the connection is back.

## Before you start

- Open Tendo and your calendar while you still have a connection. Tendo keeps a copy of the timetable and class lists it has already loaded, so it can show them offline.
- Use the same device and the same browser until your registers are sent. A register saved on one device is not on another.

## Take a register offline

1. When the connection drops, a banner appears at the top of every page. It says you are offline and can still take registers.
2. Open the lesson and mark the students as usual.
3. Select **Save register**.
4. Tendo shows **Saved on this device**. The register is now waiting to be sent.

If you save the same register again before it is sent, the new version replaces the waiting one.

## Send the waiting registers

You do not need to do anything. Tendo sends waiting registers by itself:

- as soon as the connection is back,
- when you open Tendo,
- every minute while something is waiting.

The banner shows how many registers are waiting to be sent. Once you are online, you can also select **Send now**. When the banner disappears, everything has been sent.

> **Important:** Keep Tendo open, or open it again, once you are back online. Check that the banner is gone before you sign out or hand the device to someone else.

## What does not work offline

- Logging a conduct record needs a connection.
- Staff check-in needs the internet.
- Reports and history show only what Tendo can load from the server.

## If something goes wrong

- **A register wasn't accepted.** The banner names the class, the date and the period, and gives the reason. Read the reason, select **Dismiss**, then take that register again online.
- **Registers waiting for another school.** If you belong to more than one school, a register is sent only while you are signed in to the school it was taken for. Use **Switch school** to go back to that school, and it is sent.
- **Some students were skipped.** After sending, the banner may say that students who are no longer active were left out. Select **Dismiss**.
- The class list does not load offline. Tendo can only show a class it loaded earlier on this device. Take that register when you are back online.
