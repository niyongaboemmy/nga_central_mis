---
title: Take a class register
summary: Record which students are present, late, absent or excused for a lesson.
---

Taking the register records each student's attendance for one lesson. In Tendo your calendar is the register: you open a lesson and mark it.

## Before you start

- Your lessons come from the school timetable. If a lesson is missing, see the guide on correcting or adding a register.
- The **Morning check** is the daily register of a whole class. Only the assigned class teacher takes it.

## Open the lesson

1. In the menu, select **Attendance**, then **Attendance Calendar**.
2. Choose a view: **Month**, **Week** or **Day**. Use the arrows to move, and **Today** to come back.
3. Find the lesson. The colours mean **Register taken**, **Overdue — needs attention**, **Upcoming** and **Now**.
4. In the **Day** view, select **Take register** on the lesson. In the **Week** view, point at the lesson and select **Record attendance**.

> **Tip:** Under **Your subjects**, select a subject to highlight only its lessons. Select **Clear** to show all again.

## Mark the students

1. The register opens with every student marked **Present**.
2. For each student who is not present, select **Late**, **Absent** or **Excused**.
3. Type a note next to the student if you need one, for example the reason or the minutes late.
4. To mark the whole class at once, use the buttons next to **All**.
5. In a large class, type a name in **Find a student…**.
6. Check the totals at the bottom, then select **Save register**.

After saving you see **Register saved**. If another register is waiting, select **Next:** to open it. Otherwise select **Done**.

> **Tip:** On a keyboard, move to a student's row and press 1, 2, 3 or 4 to set **Present**, **Late**, **Absent** or **Excused**.

## Overdue registers

A register is overdue when the lesson has ended and no attendance was taken. The calendar shows a banner with the number of overdue registers. Select **Take next** to open the first one.

## If something goes wrong

- **Could not save — your marks are still here, try again.** Your marks are not lost. Select **Save register** again.
- **Saved on this device.** You have no connection. Tendo sends the register by itself when you are back online.
- **No students in this class for the selected year.** Check the academic period in the top bar.
- **Some students skipped.** Students who are no longer active at the school are left out of the register.
- If you close a register with unsaved marks, Tendo asks **Discard your changes?** Select **Keep editing** to go back.
