---
title: Record a conduct incident
summary: Log a demerit or a merit for one or several students and follow it up.
---

A conduct record notes a student's behaviour. A demerit records misconduct and takes points away. A merit records good behaviour and adds points.

## Before you start

You need the right to log discipline records. If the save button stays disabled, ask your school administrator.

## Log the record

1. In the menu, select **Discipline**, then **Log Conduct Record**.
2. Under **Record type**, choose **Demerit** or **Merit**.
3. Choose the **Class**, then the **Student**.
4. To log the same record for several students, tick **Multiple students** and tick each student. **Select all** ticks the whole class.
5. Choose a **Rule** from your school's list. If no rule fits, select **Use a custom category instead**, then choose a **Category** and a **Severity** (for a merit, a **Level**).
6. Type a short **Title**, for example "Late to morning assembly".
7. Add a **Description**: what happened, the context and any witnesses.
8. Check the **Date** and add the **Location** if it helps.
9. For a demerit, choose a **Sanction**, such as **Verbal warning**, **Parent contact** or **Detention**. Leave **None** if there is no sanction.
10. Check the points shown next to **Conduct impact**.
11. Select **Save record**.

Tendo confirms and opens **Discipline Records**.

> **Note:** If your school has not set up a list of rules, you see **Category** and **Severity** straight away.

## Follow up a record

1. In the menu, select **Discipline**, then **Discipline Records**.
2. Find the record with **Search**, **Type** or **Status**.
3. Open the actions menu at the end of the row.
4. Select **Open details** to read the full record and its **History**.
5. Select **View full report** to see the student's whole report.

A demerit starts as **Open**. Staff who review discipline can select **Mark under review**, **Resolve** or **Dismiss**.

## Correct a record

In **Open details**, select **Correct details**, change what is wrong, then select **Save correction**. You can correct a record you logged during the first 24 hours. After that, ask a member of staff who manages discipline. Every correction is kept in the history.

## If something goes wrong

- **Log this again?** You already logged this incident for this student on this date. Select **Log anyway** only if it really is a second incident, because the points add up.
- **Pick a rule, or switch to "Use a custom category instead".** Choose a rule before you save.
- **A short title is required.** Fill in the **Title**.
- **Some students skipped.** Students who are no longer active at the school are left out.
