---
title: Check in and out as staff
summary: Record your own arrival and departure with your phone, and see your check-in history.
---

Staff check-in records the time you arrive at school and the time you leave. You do it on your own phone, once each school day.

## Before you start

- Your school must have turned staff check-in on.
- You need a Google account that your school knows.
- Check-in works only inside the school grounds, and it needs the internet.

## Register your phone (once)

1. In Tendo, select **Attendance**, then **Staff Attendance**.
2. Under **Check in and out with your phone**, scan the code with your phone camera.
3. On the phone, continue with your Google account.
4. Confirm that the phone is yours.
5. Select **Allow location**. Your location is read only at the moment you tap.

> **Important:** One phone belongs to one staff member. You cannot register a phone that a colleague already uses.

## Check in and check out

1. When you arrive at school, open the check-in page on your phone.
2. Wait until the page shows **At school**, then tap **Check in**.
3. Before you leave, open the page again and tap **Check out**.
4. Confirm **Check out now?** After this you cannot check in again until tomorrow.

On a computer, select **Use my phone** and scan the code shown, because computers have no GPS.

## Your working hours

Open **My calendar** on the check-in page to see your working days and hours. Checking in after the start time counts as late. Checking out before the end time counts as leaving early. The school office can change your hours.

## Reminders and history

- On the check-in page, open **Reminders** and select **Turn on** to get a notification before your check-in and check-out times.
- In Tendo, **Staff Attendance** lists your past days under **My shift history**, with **Clock in**, **Clock out** and **Status**.

## If something goes wrong

- **Not at school yet.** Check-in opens when you arrive. If you are at school, step outside or near a window and keep Wi-Fi on.
- **Weak GPS.** You can still check in. Your school confirms it afterwards.
- **You did not check out.** Enter the time you left and select **Send**. Your school confirms the time.
- **My Gmail is not recognised.** Add your Gmail in your Amashuri profile, in the staff attendance section, then try again.
- New, lost or changed phone: ask the school office to remove the old one, then register the new one.
- No smartphone: ask the school office for a staff QR card.
