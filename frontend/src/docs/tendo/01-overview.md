title: Tendo overview
summary: Learn what Tendo is for, how to open it and where to find each page.
---

Tendo is the Amashuri app for attendance and discipline. Teachers and school staff use it to take class registers, record student conduct, read attendance reports and record their own arrival and departure.

## What you can do in Tendo

- Take the register for a lesson or for the morning check. A register is the list of students in a class with a mark for each one: **Present**, **Late**, **Absent** or **Excused**.
- Correct a register, or record a session that is not on your timetable.
- Log a conduct record for a student: a demerit for misconduct or a merit for good behaviour.
- Review absence requests sent by students.
- Read and export attendance reports.
- Check in when you arrive at school and check out before you leave.

## Open Tendo

1. Go to `https://tendo.amashuri.com`.
2. Sign in with your Amashuri account.
3. You land on the **Dashboard**.

## The Dashboard

The Dashboard greets you and shows what needs your attention today.

- Your check-in card shows whether you have checked in today.
- The **Today** card lists today's lessons. Each lesson shows **Take register**, **Done**, **Overdue** or **Upcoming**. Select **Open calendar** to see the full day.
- The **Alerts** card lists things that need your attention.
- The other cards show attendance numbers and a **Conduct Overview**.

## Getting around

The menu on the left is grouped into areas. Select an area to see its pages.

| Area | Pages for staff |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Your overview page |
| **Attendance** | **Attendance Calendar**, **Attendance History**, **Attendance Report**, **Staff Attendance** |
| **Discipline** | **Log Conduct Record**, **Discipline Records**, **Excuse Review** |
| **Insights** | **Reports**, **Directory** |

You may see more or fewer pages, depending on your role at the school.

The bar at the top has more tools:

- **Apps** opens the other Amashuri apps.
- **Search** finds a page by name.
- The academic period switcher sets the academic year and term that every page shows.
- **Notifications** lists your recent alerts.
- The account menu has **Settings**, **Switch school** (if you belong to more than one school) and **Sign out**.

> **Tip:** In the menu, use **Pin to top** next to a page you open often. It then appears under **Pinned**.

> **Important:** If a page looks empty, check the academic period in the top bar first. Tendo only shows data for the year and term selected there.
