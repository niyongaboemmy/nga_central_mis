---
title: Notifications, status and signing in
summary: Choose what Tupo tells you about, set quiet hours and your status, and understand how signing in and out works.
---

Tupo can tell you about new messages, mail, posts and meetings. This guide shows how to control those alerts and how sign-in works.

## See what needs you

1. Select **Notifications** (the bell) in the top bar.
2. Filter the list with **All**, **Chat**, **Mail**, **Feed** or **Meet**. Switch between **All** and **Unread**.
3. Select an item to open it, or select **Mark all read**.

## Choose how you are notified

1. Open the **Account menu** (your photo) and select **Settings**.
2. Under **Notify me about**, choose **Everything**, **Mentions** or **Nothing**. A mention is a message that names you with `@`.
3. Under **How**, switch these on or off.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Desktop notifications** | Shows a pop-up when Tupo is in the background. |
| **Sound** | Plays a short sound for a new message. |
| **Notifications when Tupo is closed** | Alerts you even when no Tupo tab is open. |
| **Email me what I missed** | Sends an hourly e-mail with unseen mentions and direct messages. |

Your browser asks for permission the first time. Allow it.

> **Tip:** On an iPhone, add Tupo to the Home Screen first. Notifications when Tupo is closed do not work otherwise.

## Set quiet hours

1. In **Settings**, find **Quiet hours**.
2. Set **From** and **Until**.

During these hours there is no sound and no pop-up. Messages still arrive.

## Quieten one conversation

1. In **Chat**, open the actions for a conversation in the list.
2. Select **Mute…** and pick how long, for example **For 8 hours**.

You can also open **Conversation details** and choose **All**, **Mentions** or **Nothing** for that conversation only.

## Set your status

Your status tells others whether you are available.

1. Open the **Account menu**.
2. Under **Status**, choose **Automatic**, **Busy**, **Do not disturb** or **Appear offline**.

**Do not disturb** stops pop-ups and sounds. While you are in a meeting, others see "In a meeting" without you doing anything.

## Signing in and out

You do not sign in to Tupo separately. If you are signed in to Amashuri, Tupo opens without asking.

To sign out, open the **Account menu** and select **Sign out**. This signs this device out of Tupo and stops its notifications. After you sign out on purpose, Tupo waits on the sign-in screen until you choose to sign in again.

## If something goes wrong

- If **Desktop notifications** says it is blocked by the browser, allow notifications for Tupo in your browser's site settings, then switch it on again.
- If you are asked to sign in more often than expected, sign in to Amashuri first, then open Tupo again.
