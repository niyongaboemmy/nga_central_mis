---
title: Read and send mail
summary: Use Tupo Mail to write, reply to and organise messages, and connect your school mailbox.
---

Mail is for longer or more formal messages than chat. This guide covers reading, sending and connecting your school e-mail address.

## Read your mail

1. Open **Mail**. On a phone it is under **More**.
2. Choose a folder: **Inbox**, **Starred**, **Snoozed**, **Sent**, **Drafts**, **Scheduled**, **Archive**, **Spam** or **Trash**.
3. Select a message to open it.

Use **Search mail** to find a message.

With a message open you can **Star** it, **Archive** it, **Delete** it, add **Labels**, or **Snooze** it so it returns later (for example **Tomorrow**).

## Send a message

1. Select **Compose**.
2. In **To**, type a name or an e-mail address. Select **Cc/Bcc** to add more people.
3. Type a **Subject** and your message.
4. Use **Attach files** to add files.
5. Select **Send**.

Right after you send, a notice shows **Undo** for a moment. Select it if you changed your mind.

To send later, select **Send options** beside **Send**, pick a date and time under **Schedule send**, then select **Schedule**.

To finish later, select **Save draft**. The message waits in **Drafts**.

## Reply or forward

1. Open the message.
2. Select **Reply**, **Reply all** or **Forward**.
3. Write your message and send it.

## Connect your school mailbox

Connecting lets you see e-mail sent to your school address inside Tupo, and reply from that address. Tupo only reads the mailbox. Nothing there is moved, deleted or marked read.

1. In **Mail**, select **Mail settings**.
2. Under **School mailbox**, type your **Email address** and **Email password**. This is your school e-mail password, not your Amashuri one.
3. Tick **Send my replies from this address** if replies should come from your school address.
4. Select **Connect mailbox**.

Afterwards you can use **Sync now**, **Pause syncing** or **Disconnect**.

> **Note:** If the **School mailbox** section shows a message instead of the form, this feature is not available at your school.

## Other mail settings

On **Mail settings** you can also tick **Append a signature to new messages**, and choose **Deliver mail to my email inbox too**. Select **Save** when you finish.

## If something goes wrong

- **Your message was not sent**: check the addresses and your connection, then send again.
- If the mailbox shows an error after you changed your e-mail password, select **Disconnect**, then connect again with the new password.
- **Empty Trash** deletes everything in **Trash** for good. This cannot be undone.
