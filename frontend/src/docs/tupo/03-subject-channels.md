---
title: Use channels for your subjects
summary: Find the channels of each subject you teach, add a new channel and keep up with unread messages.
---

A channel is a named room where a whole class talks about one topic. The **Channels** area groups channels under each of your subjects, so a subject such as Mathematics has its own set.

## Before you start

Your subjects come from Amashuri. If you see "You have no subjects yet", your subjects have not reached Tupo. They appear after you next sign in.

## Open a subject channel

1. Open **Channels**.
2. Find your subject in the list. Under its name you see **Teaching** for subjects you teach.
3. Select the subject to show or hide its channels.
4. Select a channel to open it.

To find one quickly, type in **Find a subject or channel**.

A number beside a channel shows unread messages. A red number starting with `@` means someone mentioned you.

## Add a channel to a subject

You can add channels to subjects where you are allowed to.

1. Under the subject, select **Add channel**.
2. In **Name**, type a short name in lower case with no spaces, for example `homework`.
3. In **Topic**, you can say what the channel is for.
4. Select **Create**.

Everyone in the subject is in the new channel. You do not need to add students one by one.

> **Tip:** Keep one channel per purpose, for example `homework` for tasks and `general` for everything else. Students then know where to ask.

## Write in a channel

A subject channel works like any other chat. You can send messages, attach files, reply, pin a message and start a meeting. See the guide "Chat with people and groups" for the steps.

## Join other channels

Some channels are open for anyone to join.

1. Open **Chat**.
2. Select **Browse channels** at the top of the conversation list.
3. Search for the channel and join it.

## If something goes wrong

- "Couldn't load your subjects." means the list did not load. Select **Try again**.
- If a subject is missing or shows the wrong class, the subject assignment needs correcting in Amashuri, not in Tupo.
- If you do not see **Add channel** under a subject, you are not allowed to create channels there.
