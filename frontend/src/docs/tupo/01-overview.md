Tupo is the Amashuri app for talking to colleagues, students and the school community. This guide shows what is in it and how to move around.

## What you can do in Tupo

| Area | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Feed** | School news and announcements, posted on pages you can follow. |
| **Chat** | Direct messages, groups and channels. |
| **Channels** | One place for the channels of each subject you teach. |
| **Meet** | Video and audio meetings, started now or scheduled. |
| **Mail** | Longer, formal messages with folders, drafts and attachments. |

You only see the areas your role is allowed to open.

## Before you start

You need an Amashuri account. Tupo has no password of its own. Your name, photo and role come from your Amashuri account.

## Open Tupo

1. Sign in to Amashuri.
2. Open Tupo from the list of Amashuri apps.
3. If you are already signed in to Amashuri, Tupo signs you in by itself. Otherwise, use the sign-in button and you are brought straight back.

Tupo opens on the first area you are allowed to use, usually the **Feed**.

## Get around

On a computer, the areas are icons down the left edge. Hover over an icon to see its name. A number on an icon shows how many unread items are waiting.

On a phone, the areas are tabs along the bottom. **Mail** is under **More**.

The top bar is the same on every screen:

- **View applications** (top left) opens the other Amashuri apps.
- **Search people, channels and messages** searches all of Tupo. You can also press Ctrl+K, or Command+K on a Mac.
- **Notifications** (the bell) lists what needs your attention.
- **Switch to dark theme** or **Switch to light theme** changes the colours.
- **Account menu** (your photo) holds your **Status**, **Settings** and **Sign out**.

> **Tip:** When you are outside Chat, a **Messages** button stays on screen so you can answer a message without leaving the page you are on.

## If something goes wrong

- **Access pending** means an administrator has not given you a role in Tupo yet. Ask them, then select **Check again**.
- **Your Tupo account is suspended** means you cannot use Tupo for now. Contact the school office or your administrator.
- A bar saying **A new version of Tupo is ready.** means an update is waiting. Select **Reload**.
- A bar saying you are offline means your connection dropped. Messages you write are sent when you reconnect.
