---
title: Chat with people and groups
summary: Start a direct message or a group, reply to messages and share files in Tupo Chat.
---

Chat is for quick, everyday conversation with one person or with many. This guide covers starting a conversation, replying and sharing files.

## Start a conversation

1. Open **Chat**.
2. Select **New conversation** at the top of the conversation list.
3. Choose a tab: **Direct message** (one person), **Group** (several people) or **Channel** (a named room for a topic).
4. Search for people by name or e-mail and select them.
5. For a group, you can type a group name. For a channel, type a name such as `senior-4-science` and say what the channel is for.
6. Select **Open chat** for a direct message, or **Create** for a group or channel.

You only see the tabs your role allows.

## Send a message

1. Open the conversation and type in the message box.
2. Press Enter to send. Press Shift+Enter for a new line.

The buttons around the message box do more:

- **Attach a file** adds files. You can also drag a file onto the conversation.
- **Mention someone** types `@` so you can pick a person, who is then notified.
- **Insert emoji** adds an emoji.
- **Record a voice message** records your voice when the box is empty.
- **Schedule this message** sends it later, for example **Tomorrow, 08:00**.
- **Start or schedule a meeting** starts a video meeting with the people in the conversation.

## Reply to and manage a message

Hover over a message on a computer, or press and hold it on a phone, to see its actions.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add reaction** | Adds an emoji under the message. |
| **Reply** | Answers that message. In some conversations you see **Quote reply** and **Reply in thread** instead. A thread keeps the replies together under the first message. |
| **Save message** | Keeps it in **Saved items**. |
| **Forward message** | Sends a copy to another conversation. |
| **Pin message** | Keeps it at the top for everyone. |
| **Edit message** / **Delete message** | Changes or removes your own message. |
| **More actions** | Includes **Copy text**, **Copy link**, **Mark unread from here** and **Report**. |

## Find files and people in a conversation

1. Select **Conversation details** at the top of the conversation.
2. Use the tabs **About**, **Members**, **Files** and **Links**.

## If something goes wrong

- **An attachment did not upload**: remove the file or try again before you send.
- A message marked **Failed to send** did not go. Check your connection.
- If a message worries you, use **Report** and choose a reason such as **Bullying or harassment** or **Someone may be at risk**.
