---
title: Start, schedule and join meetings
summary: Hold a video or audio meeting in Tupo Meet, schedule one for later and invite the right people.
---

Meet is for online lessons, staff meetings and calls. This guide covers starting a meeting now, scheduling one and joining.

## Before you start

Your role decides what you can do. If you see "You can join meetings you are invited to, but not start your own", you can only join.

Your browser asks to use your camera and microphone the first time. Allow it.

## Start a meeting now

1. Open **Meet**.
2. Select **Start now**.
3. Check your camera and microphone, then select **Join now**.

By default, **Anyone signed in** with the link can join. To let in people without an account, select **Choose who can join** beside **Start now** and pick **Public**. Public guests type a name and always wait until you let them in.

## Schedule a meeting

1. In **Meet**, select **Schedule**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Starts** and **Length**. Use **Repeats** for a regular meeting, for example **Every week**.
3. Under **How people join**, choose **Private**, **Anyone signed in** or **Public**. For **Private**, search under **Who is this for?** and add the people.
4. Adjust the options you need, such as **Waiting room**, **Everyone joins muted** or **Audio only**.
5. Select **Schedule**.

The meeting appears under **Coming up** and on the calendar in **Meet**.

## Reminders

Scheduled meetings are passed to Amashuri reminders. The host and invited people are reminded there, in the way each person has chosen in Amashuri. Meetings started with **Start now** do not send reminders.

## Join a meeting

- Select the meeting under **Happening now** or **Coming up**.
- Or type the code in **Enter a meeting code** and select **Join this meeting**.

On the check screen, select **Join now**. If the meeting has a waiting room, the button says **Ask to join** and the host lets you in.

## During the meeting

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mute microphone** / **Turn camera off** | Controls your sound and video. |
| **Present your screen** | Shows your screen to everyone. |
| **Raise hand** | Tells the host you want to speak. |
| **Participants** / **Chat** | Shows who is there and the meeting chat. |
| **More** | Holds **Polls**, **Questions**, **Breakout rooms** and captions. |
| **Leave the meeting** | Leaves. The host can also choose **End for everyone**. |

## Invite more people

As the host, select **Share** on the check screen. Use **Copy link**, **Copy code** or **Calendar file**.

## If something goes wrong

- A **Private** meeting link does not work for someone who was not invited. Add them, or change who can join.
