---
title: Follow and post on the feed
summary: Read school news on the Tupo feed, react and comment, share a post to chat and publish your own.
---

The feed is where the school shares news, announcements and events. Posts are published on pages. A page is a profile for a group, such as a department or a club, that people can follow.

## Read the feed

1. Open **Feed**.
2. Scroll to read. Select **Latest** for the newest posts or **Top** for the most popular.
3. Use the menu on the left to narrow the view: **Home**, **Reels**, **Following**, **Announcements**, **Saved** and **Pages**.

When new posts arrive while you read, a button shows how many. Select it to see them.

## Follow a page

1. In the feed menu, select **Pages**.
2. Open a page and select **Follow**.

Posts from that page then appear under **Following**. Some pages show **Required** instead. Everyone follows those and you cannot unfollow them.

## React, comment and save

- Select **Like** under a post. Hold the button to choose another reaction: **Love**, **Celebrate**, **Support**, **Insightful** or **Curious**.
- Select **Comment** to read comments and write your own.
- Open **Post actions** on the post and select **Save post** to keep it under **Saved**.

## Share a post to chat

1. Under the post, select **Share**.
2. To share the address, select **Copy link**.
3. To send it to someone in Tupo, search under **Send in chat** and select **Send** beside the person or channel.

## Publish a post

You can post only on pages where you are part of the page team. If you see no box asking "What's on your mind", you cannot post yet.

1. At the top of the feed, select the "What's on your mind" box. The **Create post** window opens.
2. Choose the page to post as, and who should see the post.
3. Write your message.
4. Add extras if you need them: **Photo/video**, **Poll** or **Event**.
5. Select **Post**.

To publish later, select **Schedule**, pick a time such as **Monday 8:00**, then select **Schedule**. To keep working on it, select **Save draft**.

> **Note:** Page owners also see an **Announcement** button. An announcement is marked clearly so people notice it.

## Change or remove your post

Open **Post actions** on your post. Select **Edit post**, **Move to drafts** or **Delete post**.

## If something goes wrong

- **Could not load the feed**: select **Try again**.
- If a photo shows **Upload failed**, remove it and add it again before you post.
- If a post is not suitable for school, open **Post actions** and select **Report post**.
