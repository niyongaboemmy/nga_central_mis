---
title: Finding your way around Amashuri
summary: Learn what Amashuri is for, what staff can do in it and how to move between pages, schools and the other apps.
---

Amashuri is your school's management app. Staff use it to see their timetable, plan teaching, publish lessons, support students and report on their work.

## What you can do

- See what needs you on **Home** and your week on **Schedule**.
- Plan a term, write lesson notes and build e-learning courses.
- Run office hours and follow your mentees.
- Report on lessons, supervise student projects and get reminders.

You only see the pages your role allows. If a page named in these guides is missing, ask your school administrator.

## The menu on the left

The left edge holds a strip of icons, one per area. **Home** and **Schedule** open a page. The other icons open a panel that lists the pages of that area.

| Area | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Teaching** | Planning, your classes, office hours, mentees |
| **Workspace** | Calendar, staff cover, documents, projects |
| **Oversight** | Reviews, reports and monitoring for leaders |
| **Administration** | Users, academics and school settings |

To find a page quickly:

1. Open any area so the panel shows.
2. Type part of the page name in **Search menu**.
3. Select the page in the results.

> **Tip:** Hover over a page in the panel and select the star to pin it. Pinned pages show under **Pinned**, and pages you opened lately under **Recent**.

## Home

**Home** opens after you sign in. **Next up** shows the most urgent item, **Needs you** lists the work waiting on you and **Today** shows the day's lessons. On the side you find **Quick actions** and **Updates**.

## The top bar

- The grid button, **Systems**, opens **Amashuri Ecosystem**, where you open Task Mentor, Tendo, Tupo and the other apps your school uses.
- **My mentees** appears if you mentor students.
- **Notifications** is the bell. Select **Mark all read** to clear it.
- **Switch school** appears only if you belong to two or more schools.
- The academic year and term pickers set the period that most pages show.

## Your account

Select your picture at the bottom of the left strip to open **Account**. From there you reach **Profile**, **My activity**, **Reminders** and **Get the apps**. **Get the apps** installs the apps, including Amashuri Desktop.

To leave, open the account menu in the top bar and select **Sign out**.

## If something goes wrong

- **Home** says "We couldn't load your Home": select **Try again**.
- A page shows the wrong term: check the academic year and term in the top bar.
