---
title: Running office hours
summary: Schedule office hours, invite students each week and take the register.
---

Office hours are support sessions after the last period, where invited students come to you for extra help.

## Before you start

- Your role must include office hours. If **Office Hours** is not under **Teaching**, ask your school administrator.
- A student goes to one teacher's office hours at a time.

## Create office hours

1. In the menu, open **Teaching** and select **Office Hours**.
2. Select **New office hours**.
3. In **When & what**, choose the days, the start and end times and the dates. Add a title, a purpose, a room and the capacity.
4. Select **Continue to students**.
5. In **Students**, tick who should come and select **Invite students**.
6. Select **Review**, then **Publish**.

Publishing adds the office hours to your timetable and tells each invited student when and where to come. Select **Keep as draft** if you are not ready. Students are not told until you publish.

> **Tip:** In the student list, **Suggested** shows students with evidence that they need support, such as low results or missing work. You decide who is invited. Nobody is added automatically.

## Invite students each week

1. Open your office hours from **My office hours**.
2. Under **Students by week**, choose the week.
3. Select **Invite for this week**, or **Invite last week's group**.

Students see only the teacher, subject, days and room. They never see the reason you chose them.

If a student is busy with another teacher, select **Ask to transfer** and send a request. The other teacher chooses **Release** or **Keep**.

## Take the register

1. Open the session and select **Take register**.
2. Mark each student **Present**, **Late**, **Absent** or **Excused**. **Mark all present** fills the list in one step.
3. Select **Save register**.

> **Note:** If you are offline, the register is saved on your device and sent when you are back online.

## Change a session

On the office hours page, under **Next sessions**, you can move or cancel one date. Give a reason when you cancel. Students are told straight away, and a cancelled session never counts against them.

**End** stops the office hours and cancels future sessions.

## If something goes wrong

- **Registers still missing** on the **Today** tab lists sessions with no register. Open each one and save it.
- "Someone else saved this register while you were marking": select **Load their marks, keep mine**, check, then save again.
