---
title: Reminders and notifications
summary: Turn on reminders for lessons and deadlines, choose what you are reminded about and add your timetable to a calendar app.
---

Reminders nudge you before each lesson, deadline and meeting, even when Amashuri is closed.

## Turn reminders on

Do this once on every phone and computer you use.

1. Select your picture at the bottom of the left strip and choose **Reminders**.
2. Switch **Reminders** to **On**.
3. If the first step asks you to install the app, select **How to install** and follow it.
4. Select **Turn on notifications** and allow them when your browser asks.
5. Select **Send a test** and check that the test arrives.

> **Important:** On an iPhone or iPad you must install the app on the Home Screen first. Notifications do not work from the browser there.

## Choose what to be reminded about

Under **What to remind me about**, switch each kind on or off and set how long before it you are reminded.

| Kind | When it reminds you |
|---|---|
| **Lessons** | Before each lesson on your timetable |
| **Activities** | Assemblies, sports, clubs and other timetable events |
| **Quiz closes** | Before a quiz closes in Task Mentor |
| **Meetings** | Staff and parent meetings in Tupo |
| **Office hours** | Before office hours you run |

Two more settings help keep things calm:

- **Quiet hours**: non-urgent reminders wait until morning.
- **Morning briefing**: one summary of your day instead of many pings.

## Add your timetable to a calendar app

1. On the **Reminders** page, find **Add to your calendar app**.
2. Select **Copy** to copy your private link, or **Open in Apple Calendar / Outlook**.
3. Follow **How to add it** for your calendar app.

> **Important:** Keep the link private. Anyone with it can see your timetable. Select **New link** if it was shared. The old link then stops working.

## More ways to be reminded

Under **More ways to be reminded** you can connect **Telegram** or **Google Calendar**, and turn on **Email me when I miss an important reminder**.

## Notifications

Select the bell in the top bar to read **Notifications**. **Mark all read** clears them.

## If something goes wrong

- Notifications are blocked: open your browser's site settings for Amashuri and set notifications to allow.
- The test never arrives: check that notifications for the browser are allowed in your system settings and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is off.
- To stop reminders on one device, select **Turn off on this device**.
