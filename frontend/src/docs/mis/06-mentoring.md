---
title: Following your mentees
summary: See the students you mentor, log a session, answer their check-ins and download your mentoring report.
---

A mentee is a student your school has assigned to you for personal follow-up. Amashuri keeps their sessions, messages and wellbeing in one place.

## Before you start

Only students your school administrator assigns to you appear here. **My Mentees** shows in the menu only when you have mentees this year.

## See your mentees

1. In the menu, open **Teaching** and select **My Mentees**. You can also select the **My mentees** button in the top bar.
2. Use **Search mentees** to find a student by name, class or registration number.
3. Use the filters at the top to narrow the list.

| Filter | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Assigned** | Everyone you mentor |
| **Unread messages** | Mentees whose check-ins you have not read |
| **Due a session** | Mentees with no session in the last 3 weeks |
| **Follow-ups** | Sessions you flagged for follow-up that are not resolved |

## Log a session

1. Select a mentee to open their page.
2. Select **Log Session**.
3. Work through the four steps: **Session Info**, **Wellbeing & Discipline**, **Academic & Guidance** and **Wrap-up & Review**. Select **Next** to move on.
4. Tick **Follow-up Required** if the student needs another look at your next check-in.
5. Select **Log Session** to save.

Your entry is saved as a draft while you type. Earlier sessions appear on the mentee's page, where you can edit one or change its follow-up status to **OPEN**, **IN PROGRESS** or **RESOLVED**.

## Answer check-ins

A check-in is a short message a mentee sends you.

1. Select the **My mentees** button in the top bar, then **Check-in inbox**.
2. Filter by **All**, **Unread**, **Seen** or **Dealt with**.
3. Open a check-in and write a reply. The student gets a notification.
4. Select **Send reply**, **Approve** or **Send back**.

> **Note:** You must give a reason when you send a check-in back.

## Message a mentee

Select **Message** on the mentee's page to chat in Tupo.

## Download your report

1. On **My Mentees**, select **My Reports**.
2. Choose the period.
3. Review **Sessions** and **Mentee Reports**, then download the report.

## If something goes wrong

- "No mentees assigned yet": your school administrator has not assigned students to you.
- "This student is not one of your mentees": they may have moved to another mentor, or the assignment ended.
