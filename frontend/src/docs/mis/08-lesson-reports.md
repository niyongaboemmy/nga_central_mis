---
title: Reporting on your lessons
summary: Record what happened in each lesson and check your reporting record.
---

A lesson report tells school leadership whether a lesson was delivered and how it went. You file one for each lesson on your timetable.

## Before you start

- Check the academic term in the top bar. Reports are listed per term.
- If **Reporting** is not under **Teaching**, ask your school administrator.

## Report a lesson

1. In the menu, open **Teaching** and select **Reporting**.
2. Stay on the **Lesson Reports** tab. It shows a calendar of your lessons.
3. Select the day of the lesson. If the day has several lessons, choose one under **Select a session**.
4. Set the **Delivery Status**: **Delivered**, **Partial** or **Missed**.
5. Enter the **Attendance Count**, the number of students present.
6. Write under **Reflection / Challenges** what worked well and what did not.
7. Select **Submit Report**.

Days where every lesson is reported are marked as reported. Lessons still waiting show as **Pending**, and future ones as **Upcoming**.

> **Tip:** Use **Jump to date** to move to another date, and the **All subjects** filter to show one subject only.

## Report something that was not on the timetable

1. On the day, select **Add unscheduled activity**.
2. Pick the subject and class group.
3. Fill in the report and select **Log Activity**.

> **Note:** The subject and class group of an unscheduled activity cannot be changed after logging.

## Change a report

1. Select the reported lesson in the calendar.
2. Edit the details.
3. Select **Update Report**.

## See your record

1. On the **Reporting** page, open the **Dashboard** tab.
2. Read **Total Reports**, **Lessons Delivered** and **Reporting Trend**.
3. Scroll to **Submitted Reports** for the full list.
4. Select **Download Report** to save a copy.

The **Mentoring** tab on the same page opens your mentees. See the guide on following your mentees.

## If something goes wrong

- The calendar says "No scheduled lessons or reported activity found for this month": check the term in the top bar, and check that your lessons are on the timetable.
- "No subject assignments found for you in this term": ask your school administrator to assign your subjects.
- The submitted list asks you to select an academic term: choose the term in the top bar.
