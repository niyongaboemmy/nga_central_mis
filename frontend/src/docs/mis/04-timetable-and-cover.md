---
title: Your timetable and staff cover
summary: Read your weekly timetable, see your day at a glance and report an absence so your lessons are covered.
---

Your timetable shows every lesson you teach this term. Staff cover makes sure a lesson still has a teacher when you are away.

## See your week

1. In the menu, select **Schedule**.
2. Use **Previous week** and **Next week** to move, or **Today** to come back.
3. Use the class filter, which starts on **All Class Groups**, to show one class only.

> **Note:** If you see "No subjects scheduled for this term", your lessons have not been placed on the academic calendar yet. Ask the person who builds the timetable.

## See your day and your numbers

Select **Open Teacher Dashboard** on the **Schedule** page, or open **Teaching** and select **Teacher Dashboard**. It shows:

- **Up next today** and the rest of the day's lessons.
- Counts such as **Assigned Subjects**, **My Students** and **Periods This Week**.
- **Scheme coverage vs the calendar**, so you see where planning is behind.

## Your classes

Under **Teaching**, **My Subjects** lists the subjects assigned to you and **My Students** lists every student across your classes. If you are a class teacher, **Class Calendar** shows the timetable of your class.

## Report an absence

1. In the menu, open **Workspace** and select **Staff cover**.
2. Under **Report an absence**, set the **From** and **To** dates.
3. Choose a **Reason**.
4. Add a **Note for the cover teacher (optional)**, for example where the work is.
5. Select **Report absence**.

Your absence waits for approval by school leadership. It shows under **My absences**. Select **Withdraw** if your plans change.

## Cover a lesson

When you are asked to cover a lesson, it shows under **Lessons I'm covering** on the **Staff cover** page and in your notifications.

## For staff who manage cover

If you manage cover, the page also shows a **Cover board**.

1. Under **Waiting for approval**, select **Approve** or **Decline**.
2. For a lesson that needs a teacher, select **Find cover**.
3. Pick a free teacher and select **Assign**. The teacher is notified.

## If something goes wrong

- "Nobody is free then": no teacher is free in that period. Agree another solution with leadership.
- The timetable shows the wrong term: check the academic year and term in the top bar.
