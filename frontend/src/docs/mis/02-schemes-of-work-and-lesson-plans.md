---
title: Schemes of work and lesson plans
summary: Build the weekly plan for a subject and class, then add a lesson plan to each week.
---

A scheme of work is your week-by-week plan for one subject and one class in a term. Lesson plans hang on its weeks.

## Before you start

- A school administrator must have assigned the subject and class to you.
- Check the academic year and term in the top bar.

## Open a scheme

1. In the menu, open **Teaching** and select **Scheme Of Work**.
2. Find your subject with **Search subjects or classes...** if the list is long.
3. Select the card for the subject and class.

Each card shows how many weeks are planned. Switch **View** to **Compare** to see your coverage against the calendar.

## Create the scheme

A subject with no scheme shows **No Scheme of Work Yet** and three choices.

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Build Manually** | You add weeks, dates and topics row by row. **Auto-fill dates** sets the week dates from a start date. |
| **Generate with AI** | You upload your curriculum and the AI drafts the full weekly scheme. |
| **Import from DOCX** | You upload a scheme you already have as a Word file. |

## Work on the scheme

- Switch between **Timeline** and **Calendar** to change the view.
- Use **Edit Entry** on a week to change it, or **Insert Week Here** to add one between two weeks. The weeks after it are rescheduled.
- Select **Edit Table** to edit the scheme in a table that matches the printed version.
- Select **Download PDF** to get the scheme as a file.

## Add a lesson plan

1. On the week, select **Add Lesson**.
2. Choose **Build Manually**, **Generate with AI** or **Import from DOCX**.
3. Fill in the plan. The time slot and class are pre-filled from your timetable. Change them if this lesson differs.
4. Save the plan.

> **Note:** A lesson plan needs a date and a time slot, a big question and at least one titled learning outcome before it is complete.

## Review

A reviewer checks your scheme. The card then shows **Awaiting review**, **Approved** or **Sent back**. Select **Read feedback** to see the reviewer's comment.

> **Important:** An approved scheme is locked for editing.

## If something goes wrong

- **No subjects assigned**: you have no subject assignments for this academic year. Ask your school administrator.
- **Delete Scheme** removes every weekly entry and its lesson plans. Use it only to start over.
